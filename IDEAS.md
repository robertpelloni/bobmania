# Aggressive Ideas & Future Pivots

## 1. Decentralized Song Distribution
Transition from a centralized download server to a full IPFS-backed content swarm. Songs are addressed by their hash, ensuring immutability and permanent availability.

## 2. Zero-Knowledge Proofs for Scoring
Use ZK-proofs (like SP1) to prove a score was achieved on a specific chart with specific mods without revealing the full replay, enabling trustless online leaderboards.

## 3. AI-Generated Charts
Integrate an on-device LLM or specialized neural network to generate high-quality charts for any MP3/OGG on the fly, similar to "Auto-Gen" but with professional-level rhythmic awareness.

## 4. Cross-Game Asset Portability
Allow skins and avatars earned in "Bob's Game" or other partner titles to be used as NoteSkins or Backgrounds in StepMania via the Unified Asset Sync.

## 5. Web-Based Spectator Client
A WASM-based client that allows anyone with a browser to watch live StepMania matches in 3D, with real-time heart rate and input overlays.
